# IRELAND HOUSING DATA

A free, source-based housing and property information platform for Ireland.

## Purpose

IRELAND HOUSING DATA is being built to help people understand Irish housing and property law, procedures, rights, obligations, official routes and housing data.

The platform is designed around:

**Question → Facts → Classification → Law → Procedure → Deadline → Official Route → Evidence**

## Legal-information principles

- Primary legal sources take priority.
- Current and historical law are distinguished.
- Enactment and commencement are treated separately.
- Amendments and transitional provisions are tracked.
- Legal rules are separated from government guidance and administrative procedure.
- Important propositions are connected to their evidence chain.
- No unverified legal proposition is published as established law.
- The platform does not replace professional legal advice where appropriate.

## Website direction

The public interface uses:

**PURE BLACK + PURE WHITE + RESTRAINED WHITE LIGHT**

The design is intended to feel like clarity emerging from darkness: serious, calm, precise and accessible.

## Architecture

The website will progressively connect to:

- Legal proposition registry
- Legal evidence ledger
- Legal change monitor
- Legal time machine
- Legal rule composition engine
- Legal scenario engine
- Legal state machine
- Deadline engine
- Notice validator
- Tenancy classifier
- Property interest resolver
- Who-enforces-this engine
- Official procedure and application routes
- Housing data and definitions
